# Mission

Become interview-ready for SDE1 and SDE2 backend roles after a September 2026 layoff, using existing experience in C#, .NET, AWS SQS, event-driven systems, microservices, database migrations, concurrency, and distributed systems.

The immediate goal is not to collect more notes. It is to turn existing knowledge into reliable interview performance: clarify ambiguous requirements, produce a correct core design, explain trade-offs, handle concurrency and failures, and communicate at the expected level under time pressure.

## Success Criteria

- Complete a 45-60 minute LLD or machine-coding discussion with a working core design and explicit edge cases.
- Complete a 45-60 minute HLD discussion from requirements and estimates through APIs, data model, scaling, and failure handling.
- Explain when an answer is sufficient for SDE1 and what extra depth raises it to SDE2.
- Correct the highest-risk gaps found in the repository audit: executable correctness, invariants, transaction boundaries, failure recovery, and end-to-end HLD structure.
- Sustain preparation without burnout: about 90 focused minutes on normal days, with two lighter consolidation days in each two-week cycle.

## Working Principles

1. Attempt before receiving a solution.
2. Teach concepts just in time, based on mistakes or uncertainty exposed by the attempt.
3. Prefer depth on representative problems over broad note collection.
4. Treat communication, assumptions, and trade-off reasoning as part of correctness.
5. Use lightweight verification appropriate for interviews; create formal test projects only when they add clear learning value.
